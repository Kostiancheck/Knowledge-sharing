# Ship of Theseus

Dota is one of the best examples of the [Theseus's Paradox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ship_of_Theseus) for me, because I actually wintessed it myself as it was unfolding. I don't think there is a _single_ element in the game that remained unchanged since its first release in 2003. Admittedly, I was too young to be playing Dota back then. I believe the first version I played was 6.49c (2008), so I was quite late to the WC3 era DotA party.

## What's gone

Even if we take 2008 as a starting point, remarkably little has remained since. It is now a different game (literally), different game engine, different developer, different publisher. But even if we ignore Dota 1 -> Dota 2 transition, it is really hard to find anything that hasn't changed.

Literally every hero had at least some (major) changes. For instance, there are only 6 heroes that _still_ have the same core abilities: Lion, Shadow Shaman, Earthshaker, Lina, Vengeful Spirit and Crystal Maiden. Let's not take into account Shard, Aghanim's, innate abilities and talents for a moment. So if Lion mainer jumped in time from 2008 to today, they would still know how stun works. Or would they? They would try to stun a hero with Linken's Sphere, and that hero wouldn't get stunned. Lion mainer would be very confused. "Why didn't it work? I casted it on the ground!!!" they would say. Yea, not a case anymore. Impale is kind of the same, but at the same not really, because the world changed.

It's like if you wore the same jeans from 2008 today. Yes, they are still the same, they look the same, they feel the same, but they are not cool anymore. In a fashion sense, they are not the same. The world changed.

Even if we rule out 'The world changed' argument for a moment, still, literally every item, ability, creep, building in a game has changed at least a bit.

The point is, even if we are very strict with our definition of what constitutes change, Dota has completely changed. It's a whole new ship at this point. Not a single plank remains.

## What's left

One could argue that sure concrete details might've changed, but abstract ideas and relationships are still the same. It is still a game about two teams of 5 people each coming together in an effort to destroy each other's Ancient (both the teams and the Ancient were called differently back then, but whatever). That is correct. On the hero level, Lion is still a nuker/disabler. His role in the game is mostly unchaged, and role is an abstract concept.

This is not the main point of the article, but I also find it interesting that some of the _frequencies_ hasn't changed. Runes spawn every 2 minutes, creepwaves spawn every 30 seconds, netural creeps spawn every minute. 

Now, let's get back to abstract concepts. The problem that I have with abstractions here is that they are high level enough to prevent me from defying Dota.

So, abstractions let me say something like "In Dota, each team consists of 5 players". This statement has been true throughout the entirety of Dota lifetime. However, it does not help me define Dota much. I can't say "Dota is a game with two teams of 5 players each", because it is also describes CS and basketball.

That means I need to be more specific with my definition. Something like: "Dota is a game where two teams of 5 players each try to destroy enemies' Ancient", but that also covers LoL, HoN, HotS etc.

I can't even say "Dota is a game developed by Valve where two teams of 5 players each try to destroy enemies' Ancient", because it is not true. We would need to cover the fact that Dota was a map in WC3 somehow.

I can get even more specific and say "Dota is a game where two teams of 5 players each try to destroy enemies' Ancient and where you can play as Lion", since we already established that Lion is one of the most stable characters in Dota, but even this statement is not true! [HoN](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0CfqXRJ-L64) has virtually the same character.

And it goes without saying that any definition like this would be brittle and there could very well be a version of Dota in the future that is not covered by it.
