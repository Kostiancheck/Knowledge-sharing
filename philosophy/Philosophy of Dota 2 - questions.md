# Philosophy of Dota 2

If I were to write an essay about philosophy of (in?) Dota 2, what kind of questions would I raise?

The best way to live is obsession. Dota occupies 100% of brain power, regardless of how much of it you have. Obsession eliminates the 'Why?'.

Fear of death paralyzes. If you have no buy back available, you tend to play more cautiously. If you do, you take more risks. Fear of failure?

Team cooperation. You literally can not win alone, no matter how good you are. You can't even know what's going on in the game at any given moment, so you have to rely on team sharing information.

Wealth inequality. Supports virtually give gold to their carry so that they can become more powerful quicker. This tactic is so widespread because it works. In real world, this kind of works to. Giving up power basically eliminates internal struggle, allowing for bigger/more powerful armies. That's why average size of country grows.

Maximizing is not always fun. Even if you know an optimal way to play a certain hero or situation, sometimes you will play it differently just because you can. For the same reason, sometimes you will eat an entire chocolate bar or stay up too late watching a new TV show or whatever. Because maximizing even your health is not fun.
