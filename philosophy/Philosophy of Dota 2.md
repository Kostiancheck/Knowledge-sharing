# Dota is a game about money

If you were forced to predict an outcome of a random Dota game based on a single metric, which one would you choose? I would argue that money (gold) is the most important one.

The reason for this is that, much like in real life, individual (hero) is limited, while market (shop) is not.

In real life, you get 24 hours per day. Add a sleep tax to that, and that leaves you with only so much time that you can utilize. You can master a musical instrument, train to run a marathon in under 3 hours, get Radiant in Valorant, but you cannot do ALL of it. Meanwhile, if you have money, you can hear a performance of the best in class musician, drive a lambo, watch VCT finals, and even do all of it in one day. I know, this is a silly comparison, but the point still stands. Time is always limited, money is not.

That is why hero level has a limit, but net worth is (virtually) unlimited.

And coming back to dota, in a 1-1 encounter level 1 crystal maiden will ALWAYS win level 30 ursa, if cm has unlimited gold and ursa has none.

One of the most intersting things you can buy in Dota, is your own life. What makes it even more interesting is that you can know in advance how much money you are going to need to come back from the dead if you die. This enables player to take more risks once they have a buyback ready, which leads to more fun, active and interesting games.

In LoL for instance there is no buybacks. In the late game, every death can end the game, so people are very very careful with their moves.

# Angle 1 - Zero-sum Game

Dota is a zero sum game. If Radiant wins, Dire loses; and vice versa.

# Angle 2 - Money

Dota is a game about money. Money is a powerful construct. It is literally useless by itself, and yet arguably the most valuable thing you can own.

# Angle 3 - Bad decision is better than indecision

In Dota, you need to constantly make decisions when short on time. 

# Angle 4 - Fear of death

What makes Dota (and many other games) fun, is that death is temporary there. We can argue that death is temporary in the real world too, but even if so we can never really experience reincarnation.

Buyback! This is probably one of the most unique concepts in Dota. If you die, and if you have enough gold, you can buy your life back. More over, you can know in advance how much money you will need to buy back in case you die. This changes life completely. Without fear of death, you can take much much greater risk. Needless to say, greater risks are fun! If you ever saw a high profile game of League of Legends, every death there is extremely punishing to the team, especially in the late game. Sometimes, 1 kill might mean that the game is over. This leads to very long and passive games.

# Angle 5 - Defence is easier than offence

"Лучшая защита - это нападение" - this phrase is widely misunderstood. People think it means that you should attack first instead of waiting for your opponent to strike (aka "Если драка неизбежна, бить надо первым"). This is false. I believe it is just used this way to justify aggression, and it has nothing to do with efficiency at war.

If you actually want to win a war efficiently, you need to play defence, and do targeted counter-attacks at the best possible moments.
